DISCOVER SPAIN

A colorful mix of culture, coast and city life.

Spain is known for its lively cities, beautiful beaches, historic architecture and diverse regional cultures. From Barcelona and Madrid to the Mediterranean coast, travelers can enjoy food, art, history and outdoor experiences.

PLACES TO VISIT

Popular Places in Spain

CITY

Barcelona

Discover Gaudí architecture, lively streets, beaches and Catalan culture.

CAPITAL

Madrid

Explore grand boulevards, museums, parks, historic neighborhoods and Spanish cuisine.

COAST

Costa del Sol

Enjoy sunny beaches, coastal towns and beautiful Mediterranean scenery.
