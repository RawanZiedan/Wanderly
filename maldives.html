DISCOVER THE MALDIVES

A tropical escape surrounded by turquoise water.

The Maldives is a tropical paradise made up of hundreds of islands surrounded by crystal-clear lagoons and coral reefs. It is famous for its beautiful beaches, luxury resorts, romantic escapes and incredible underwater experiences.

PLACES TO VISIT

Popular Places in the Maldives

CAPITAL

Malé

Explore the lively capital and experience local markets, mosques and Maldivian culture.

ISLANDS

North Malé Atoll

Discover beautiful resorts, coral reefs, beaches and unforgettable ocean views.

UNDERWATER WORLD

South Ari Atoll

A great area for diving, snorkeling and discovering the colorful marine life.

TRAVEL SMART

Maldives Travel Tips

🚤

Choose Your Transfer

Check whether your resort requires a speedboat or seaplane transfer from Malé.

🧴

Pack Sun Protection

Bring sunscreen, sunglasses, light clothing and anything you need for tropical weather.

💳

Check Resort Policies

Review what your resort includes before booking meals, transfers and activities.

🐠

Respect Marine Life

Follow local guidelines and avoid damaging coral reefs or disturbing marine animals.
