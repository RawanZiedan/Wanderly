✦ DISCOVER INDONESIA

A world of islands & adventure.

Indonesia is a spectacular island nation known for tropical beaches, beautiful landscapes, ancient temples and diverse cultures.

From the peaceful rice terraces of Ubud to the beaches of Bali and the energy of Jakarta, Indonesia offers a huge variety of experiences.

📍 PLACES TO VISIT

Popular places in Indonesia

🏝️ Bali

Bali

Enjoy beautiful beaches, temples, waterfalls, tropical landscapes and unforgettable island experiences.

🌿 Ubud

Ubud

Discover rice terraces, traditional temples, art, local culture and peaceful natural surroundings.

🌆 Jakarta

Jakarta

Experience Indonesia's busy capital, shopping centers, local food and modern city life.

✨ EXPERIENCES

Things to do in Indonesia

🏖️

Enjoy Tropical Beaches

Relax on beautiful beaches, swim in clear water and discover peaceful tropical islands.

🛕

Visit Ancient Temples

Discover impressive temples and experience the rich cultural heritage of Indonesia.

🍜

Taste Indonesian Food

Try nasi goreng, satay, rendang and other delicious Indonesian specialties.

🌋

Explore Nature

Discover volcanoes, waterfalls, jungles, rice fields and spectacular natural landscapes.

☀️ PLAN YOUR TRIP

Best time to visit Indonesia

Indonesia has a tropical climate, and the best travel period can vary depending on the island and region.

🌸 Spring A good period for exploring many destinations.
☀️ Summer Popular for Bali, beaches and outdoor adventures.
🍂 Autumn Tropical weather with opportunities for sightseeing.
❄️ Winter Warm weather remains common across many islands.
💡 TRAVEL SMART

Indonesia travel tips

🗺️

Plan Island Transfers

Indonesia is made up of many islands, so plan flights, boats and local transportation in advance.

💳

Carry Payment Options

Keep some Indonesian Rupiah available while also carrying a card for larger purchases.

📱

Stay Connected

A local SIM or eSIM can help with navigation, translation and bookings.
