DISCOVER THAILAND

Where tropical beauty meets rich culture.

Thailand is one of Southeast Asia's most popular destinations, offering a mix of tropical beaches, lively cities, ancient temples and delicious food. From the energy of Bangkok to the peaceful islands of Phuket and the cultural charm of Chiang Mai, Thailand has something for every traveler.

PLACES TO VISIT

Popular Places in Thailand

CITY

Explore colorful temples, busy markets, modern shopping malls and vibrant nightlife.

ISLAND

Enjoy beautiful beaches, island trips, clear water and unforgettable sunsets.

CULTURE

Chiang Mai

Discover ancient temples, mountain landscapes, local markets and traditional Thai culture.

TRAVEL SMART

Thailand Travel Tips

🗺️

Plan Your Route

Combine Bangkok with an island or northern destination depending on your travel style.

💳

Carry Payment Options

Keep some Thai Baht with you while also using cards where they are accepted.

📱

Stay Connected

Consider a local SIM or eSIM so you can easily use maps and travel apps.

🧴

Protect Yourself From the Sun

Bring sunscreen, comfortable clothes and plenty of water when exploring outdoors.
